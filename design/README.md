# Design sources

Original artwork kept out of `public/`, so it isn't deployed with the site.

- `pose-scenes/`: full-size pose scenes (PNG), the morning and evening
  versions, and the background artwork they were composited from. The site
  and apps show the compressed copies in `public/poses_with_background/web/`;
  export a new or changed scene there as WebP.
- `pose-photos/`: full-size white-background pose photos (PNG). The site
  shows the compressed JPG copies in `public/poses/`, named as in the
  Practice Library sheet's `image` column.
