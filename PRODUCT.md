# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Wellness seekers building a daily ritual. Returning users who open PranaVeda as part of their morning or evening routine — not to explore, but to practice. They come with a feeling or symptom and leave with a personalized yoga sequence, breathwork pattern, and Ayurvedic guidance. The app is a quiet companion, not a discovery tool.

## Product Purpose

PranaVeda is an AI-powered Ayurvedic wellness coach. It takes how a user feels right now and synthesizes a complete, personalized daily ritual — yoga poses, breathwork cycles, dietary tips, and lifestyle advice — grounded in Ayurvedic dosha principles and delivered with the calm authority of a trusted guide. Success looks like a user who returns every day because the ritual feels effortless and the guidance feels personal.

## Brand Personality

Calm, sacred, intelligent.

Minimalist editorial illustration inspired by Apple Health, Oura, Calm, and Neo Mirai design aesthetics. The product feels like a premium health instrument — precise, unhurried, and quietly confident. Not mystical. Not clinical. Somewhere between a beautifully designed health app and an editorial wellness journal.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS / dashboard**: No metric cards, hero numbers, sidebar nav, or CTA-heavy layouts. This is not a productivity tool.
- **Fast/energetic fitness apps**: No aggressive gradients, pump-up motion, countdown urgency, or gym-app energy.
- **AI chatbot interface**: Should never feel like a chat thread or a bot response window. The AI is a coach, not a conversational agent.
- **Fantasy / mystical visual language**: No temples, mountains, sacred geometry, mandala overlays, glowing orbs, or heavy spiritual iconography. These compete with the product content and undermine the intelligent register. Ayurvedic wisdom is expressed through precision and restraint, not decoration.
- **Generic wellness app stock imagery**: No lotus flowers, green smoothies, or stock yoga photography.

## Design Principles

1. **The interface disappears** — Like Oura, the product gets out of the way. Every screen exists to surface the user's wellness ritual, not to showcase the UI. Decoration that competes with content is a bug.
2. **Restraint is the brand** — Silence, white space, and typographic hierarchy carry the aesthetic. If an element doesn't have a clear job, it doesn't ship.
3. **Ancient intelligence, modern precision** — Ayurvedic concepts expressed through sharp type, considered spacing, and clean data presentation — never through iconography or ornament.
4. **Motion that breathes** — Animations are slow, purposeful, and body-aware. Inspired by breathing exercises and circadian rhythms, not interface transitions. Every animation has a reduced-motion fallback.
5. **Data as ritual** — Wellness metrics and AI outputs are presented with the same care Apple Health gives to health data: readable, trustworthy, never overwhelming.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum. Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against all backgrounds.
- All breathing animations and ambient motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Voice input (VoiceModal) must be fully keyboard-accessible as a fallback.
- Color is never the sole carrier of meaning — dosha types and semantic states use labels alongside color.
