PranaVeda is a calm daily companion for yoga, breathwork and Ayurveda. Tell Amrita, your guide, how you feel right now, and she shapes a short ritual for this moment: a few yoga poses, a breathing practice and simple Ayurvedic advice.

A RITUAL MADE FOR TODAY
• Say or type how you feel: tired, wired, stiff, scattered, or ready to wind down.
• Amrita takes into account the time of day, the season and the weather where you are, and, if you allow it, last night's sleep and your resting heart rate.
• Each ritual comes with clear instructions for every pose, how long to hold it, and why it helps.

PRACTISE ALONG
• A guided practice takes you through each pose with a timer and spoken cues.
• Breathwork with a gentle visual guide for every inhale, hold and exhale.

KNOW YOURSELF
• Discover your dosha, your Ayurvedic constitution, through a short conversation.
• Amrita remembers what matters, such as an old injury, and adapts poses to keep you safe.
• A practice calendar and trends show how your practice and your sleep move together.

FITS AROUND YOUR DAY
• If you turn them on, gentle reminders when you arrive at a place you saved, such as home.
• Optional: connect your calendar so Amrita can suggest practice in your free time. She sees only when you're busy, never what your events are.
• Home screen widgets for your next practice.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account needed. Sign in with Google only if you want your profile on more than one device.
• Your health readings, location and calendar stay on your device.
• No ads, no tracking, no selling of your data.
• Delete your account and everything kept with it at any time.

PranaVeda offers general wellness guidance, not medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new practice, especially if you are pregnant or have a health condition.
