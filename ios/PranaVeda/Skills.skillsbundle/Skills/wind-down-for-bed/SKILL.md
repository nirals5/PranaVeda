# Winding down for bed

In PranaVeda, winding down means a short, gentle practice: slow breathing or
restful yoga, about 5 to 20 minutes, with nothing energising. It is never a
strong flow, sun salutations, backbends or fast breathing.

Look at the user's saved rituals first. A ritual suits winding down when its
title, poses or description point to evening, sleep, rest, calm or gentle
movement, such as legs up the wall, child's pose, a reclined twist or
corpse pose. If one suits, use the Start My Practice tool with that ritual.
If more than one suits, pick the shortest, or ask if the user has said how
much time they have.

If no saved ritual suits, don't start an energising one in its place. Use the
Ask Amrita tool, and ask her for a short wind-down practice for bed with slow
breathing and a calm reflection. Pass on anything the user said about how
they feel or how much time they have, in their own words.

## Don't

- Don't start a practice the user hasn't asked for. If they only said they
  feel tired or stressed, offer the wind-down and wait for a yes.
- Don't give medical or sleep-disorder advice yourself. Amrita answers
  questions about health and Ayurveda.
- Don't use the Practice Summary tool here unless the user asks about their
  streak.
