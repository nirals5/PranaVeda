Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. Signing in with Apple or Google only keeps your wellness profile, practice log and saved rituals the same on the website and your other devices.

How do I delete my account?

In the iPhone or Android app, go to You and tap Delete account. On the website, open your profile and choose Delete account. Your account and everything kept with it are deleted straight away. Details are in the privacy policy.

Amrita can't hear me

Voice needs permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. On iPhone, check Settings → PranaVeda. On Android, check the app's permissions in Settings. On the website, allow the microphone in your browser's site settings; voice works in Chrome, Edge and Safari. You can always type instead.

My sleep and heart readings don't show

PranaVeda reads them from Apple Health on iPhone and from Health Connect on Android, only with your permission. Check that PranaVeda is allowed to read sleep, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability there, and that your watch or ring writes them. Readings stay on your device.

How do I see my heart rate on Apple Watch?

Start a guided practice on your iPhone. PranaVeda opens on your Apple Watch and shows your heart rate until you end the practice.

Is this medical advice?

No. PranaVeda offers general wellness guidance, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Check with your doctor before starting a new practice, especially if you are pregnant or have a health condition, and stop any pose that hurts.
