PranaVeda

Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

PranaVeda is a yoga, breathwork and Ayurvedic wellness companion with a guide called Amrita. It is made by Hetan Shah. This policy covers the PranaVeda website, the iPhone and Apple Watch apps (with their widgets), the Android app, and the PranaVeda Alexa skill, which we call "PranaVeda" here.

In short

  • You can use PranaVeda without an account. An account is optional and only keeps your things in step across your devices.
  • We do not show ads, sell your information, track you across other apps or websites, or use analytics.
  • Your health readings, precise location and calendar stay on your device. Amrita sees only short summaries, and only when you choose.
  • To answer you, what you say to Amrita is sent to Anthropic, the company that provides the AI model. Your conversations are not stored on our servers.
  • You can delete your account and everything kept with it at any time from the app or the website.

What we collect and why

When you talk to Amrita

Your messages go to our server and on to Anthropic so that Amrita can answer. With them we send what Amrita needs to suggest the right practice:

  • your wellness profile (see below);
  • your local time and date;
  • a summary of your recent practice;
  • if you connected a calendar, only the times you are busy, never what your events are;
  • if you saved places such as Home or Work, only which kind of place you are at, never where it is;
  • the weather where you are (see "Approximate location");
  • if you allowed it, a short summary of your sleep and heart readings (see "Health and fitness data").

We do not store your conversations on our servers. The chat stays on your device.

Your wellness profile

As you talk, Amrita keeps a profile so that her guidance fits you. It can include the name you give her, your dosha (Ayurvedic constitution), your experience with yoga, your goals and preferences, health conditions you mention (such as an injury or pregnancy, so she can avoid unsuitable practices), and short notes from your conversations. Without an account it stays on your device. With an account it is kept with your account so the website and your other devices share it.

Your account

An account is optional. You sign in with Google or with Apple, and we receive your name, your email address (with Apple you can hide it, and Apple gives us a private relay address instead) and an identifier for your Google or Apple account. We keep these, your timezone, and, for each device you sign in on, its type, operating system, app or browser version and when it last used PranaVeda, so you can see and end your sessions. With Sign in with Apple we also keep, encrypted, a token from Apple, used only to tell Apple when you delete your account.

While you are signed in, your account also keeps:

  • your practice log: which rituals and breathwork you did and when, and how you felt afterwards if you said;
  • the rituals you saved;
  • if you turn it on, your saved places and the website's calendar link, encrypted. This is off by default; until you turn it on they never leave your device.

Health and fitness data

With your permission, PranaVeda reads sleep, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability from Apple Health (iPhone), Health Connect (Android) or the Google Health API (website, for Fitbit and Pixel Watch), and live heart rate from a Bluetooth heart-rate monitor you connect on the website. It uses them to draw your Heart & sleep trends beside your practice days. These readings are kept on your device. We never send your daily readings to our servers or to anyone else, and PranaVeda never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.

If you choose to let Amrita see your readings ("Let Amrita see my sleep and heart readings" in the apps, or by connecting a device on the website), a short summary, such as last night's sleep against your usual, goes with your messages to Amrita so she can take it into account. It is not stored. Health data is never used for advertising or sold.

Location

Precise location. If you allow it, your device's location is compared with places you saved yourself (Home, Work and so on), on your device. Only the kind of place, such as "home", is ever shared with Amrita. Your position is not sent to us and no location history is kept.

Approximate location. Our hosting provider estimates your city from your network address. We use it, rounded to about 10 km, to look up the local weather from Open-Meteo, so that Amrita does not suggest a cooling breath on a cold day. It is not stored.

Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar or a private calendar link, PranaVeda reads only when you are busy, to suggest practice in your free time. Event titles and details are never read. Your calendar is not stored on our servers, except the calendar link itself, encrypted, if you choose to keep it on your account.

Voice

If you speak to Amrita, your device's speech recognition (Apple's or Google's, or your browser's) turns your voice into text; we receive only the text. "Hey Amrita" listens on your device only and sends nothing until it hears its name. If Amrita speaks her replies in her own voice, the text of each reply is sent to ElevenLabs to make the audio. No recordings are kept.

The Alexa skill

The Alexa skill does not link to a PranaVeda account and knows nothing about you. Amazon sends us the words of your request so we can answer. If you ask for reminders, Amazon asks for your permission and keeps the reminder.

Technical information

To keep PranaVeda working and protect it from abuse, our servers count requests for a short time, by account or, for guests, by network address; the counts are deleted within a day. Our hosting provider keeps standard request logs (such as network address, time and page) for a limited time.

Cookies and storage on your device

The website uses cookies only to keep you signed in. It stores your preferences, chat, profile and trends in your browser's storage, and the apps keep them on your device, so PranaVeda works without an account. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and what is stored with it until you delete your account. Information kept only on your device stays there until you remove it or the app. Messages to Amrita, weather lookups and health summaries are used to answer you and are not kept by us. Our providers keep information as their own policies describe.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time: in the iPhone or Android app under You → Delete account, or on the website in your profile. Your account, profile, practice log, saved rituals, saved places and sessions are deleted immediately, and if you used Sign in with Apple we tell Apple to revoke PranaVeda's access.
  • Stop sharing: turn off "Let Amrita see my sleep and heart readings", disconnect your calendar or devices, or turn off keeping places on your account.
  • Withdraw permissions for health data, location, microphone and speech recognition in your device's settings, or in Health Connect on Android. On iPhone you can also stop using your Apple ID with PranaVeda in Settings → Apple Account → Sign in with Apple.
  • See, correct or get a copy of your information, or ask any question about it, by writing to us at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to your data protection authority.

Security

PranaVeda uses encrypted connections everywhere. Sign-in tokens, your saved places and your calendar link are stored encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

PranaVeda is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Not medical advice

PranaVeda offers general wellness guidance, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Readings from your devices are wellness signals, not medical measurements.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information we already have, we will tell you in the apps or on the website first.
